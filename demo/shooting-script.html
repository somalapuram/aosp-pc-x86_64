Four claims cut or corrected before you film

An adversarial review of earlier drafts caught these. Each would have failed a fact-check by exactly the engineer you are trying to reach — and the whole pitch rests on being checkable.

  • 9.4× → 9.2×599/65 = 9.22, but 30.303/3.226 = 9.39. Putting both raw numbers on screen and then asserting 9.4× invites a division that disagrees with your own caption. The script now shows the frame counts and says 9.2×, then names the fps figure separately.
  • cut“The GPU clock never moved before.” Unsupported. config/pc_x86_64.fragment:159 records that 400 MHz “is all this workload asks of it”, and GuC SLPC owns the frequency. Claiming it as a win invites “so your GPU is 82 % idle.” Replaced with the honest version, which is stronger.
  • reframed“AMD doesn’t work.” Untested. build-mesa.sh:172 passes -Dllvm=disabled and builds only iris,virgl — radeonsi requires LLVM. Nothing was tried on AMD hardware and found broken; the driver was never built, and there is no AMD machine in the project. Saying otherwise is both wrong and rude to the people you want help from.
  • movedThe why-should-I-care now lands at 0:35 rather than 1:40. A platform director stops watching before a boot sequence finishes.

6:358:05

Three asks

One card per company, each held while you speak it. Same layout, same rhythm, so the three read as a set.

Ask one

Google

Take the audio HAL patch. It is small, it is upstream-shaped, and it fixes a real bug for any device whose microphones are not on ALSA device zero — not just this one.

Then tell me whether a bare-metal x86_64 target belongs in AOSP. There is no maintained one. This device tree is 265 files against stock android17-release. If that is interesting, I would rather maintain it in the open than in a fork.

Ask two

Intel

Your GPU already works. Iris drives Meteor Lake at GLES 3.2 on this machine and it is 9.2× the software path. The GSC and HuC firmware path authenticates for all workloads.

What I want is validation and the parts I cannot see. Thirty frames per second against a sixty hertz panel says this is capped, not saturated. I do not know whether the remaining gap is composition, power management, or something in the media path — and you do.

Ask three

AMD

This is the concrete one. Your GPUs have no hardware path here, and that is a gap in my build, not a fault in your hardware. Three things stand between here and working: DRV_AMDGPU in minigbm, libdrm_amdgpu, and a cross-built radeonsi with LLVM enabled.

The Intel path took days once the shape was clear. If someone who already knows that stack wants to point me at the right approach — or if there is a machine I could test on — I will do the work and publish it.

Before you roll

  • Two capture sources: a locked-off camera on the ZBook, and clean screen/adb capture from a second machine. Never film a screen when a clean capture exists.
  • Terminal at 16–18 pt minimum, dark background, no transparency, prompt reduced to $ . Type commands live; never pre-scroll.
  • Use whatever your machine actually prints. If output differs from this script, the machine is right and the script is wrong.
  • Record the two benchmark runs separately and place them side by side in the edit — then say so on screen, as at 1:40.
  • No music under the cold open or the split screen. Both work because they are unglamorous.
  • You cannot screen-record on the device. screenrecord segfaults in CCodec::createInputSurface — the Codec2 HAL has no encoder that provides an input surface — which also rules out scrcpy. Capture the screen externally: HDMI into a capture card, or film the panel. Stills via adb exec-out screencap work perfectly and are what the reference frames in this script came from.
